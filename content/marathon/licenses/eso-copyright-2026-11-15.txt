We understand that the most appropriate way of doing so may vary from case to case. See the Q&A below for some common examples. Q: I want to use the footage in a YouTube video. Is it OK just to put the credit in the text description? A: No, as this is hidden inside a tab and therefore not visible as we request. Also it is not visible if the video is embedded in another website. In the special case where you cannot credit the video visibly, please include the credit line ‘burned in’ or overlaid on the video, or clearly visible as a credit at the end. Q: I want to upload an ESO show to my YouTube channel, or embed this in my blog. Do I need to add a credit ‘burned in’ or overlaid on the video? A: If the video in question (e.g. ELT Update or ESO Chasing Starlight) already has a credit at the end, so you do not need to edit it.
